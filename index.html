Who We Help

Built for the Firms Delivering AI and Hyperscale Infrastructure

Hyperscalers may be the end-market. They are not always the first client. Arcline serves the broader ecosystem — the firms building, commissioning, financing, and operating hyperscale-grade infrastructure. If your team is exposed when something goes wrong on a high-voltage data center project, we are built for you.

01
EPCs & Design-Build Firms

Independent review of protection coordination, arc flash studies, commissioning risk, and utility interface assumptions before issues reach the owner. We help EPC teams de-risk deliverables — especially at the MV/HV boundary where most data center failures originate.

02
Colocation Operators & Data Center Developers

Hyperscale-grade electrical infrastructure advisory for teams expanding capacity, evaluating new sites, or preparing for energization — without needing a full internal power systems team. We bring the owner-side operational judgment that most consultants lack.

03
Commissioning Firms

Senior MV/HV power-system support behind your commissioning program — relay setting validation, energization sequencing, UPS bypass planning, generator paralleling, and live-site cutover risk review. We are not a replacement for your Cx team. We are the technical layer that protects it.

04
Utilities & Interconnection Teams

Customer-side data center load expertise for large-load interconnections, power quality concerns, protection coordination alignment, and AI/GPU load behavior. We help utilities understand what hyperscale operators actually need — before and after the meter.

05
Infrastructure Investors & Owner's Representatives

Technical due diligence on electrical capacity, reliability exposure, protection gaps, expansion readiness, and operational risk. We give infrastructure investors and owner's reps the same independent technical lens that hyperscale operators apply internally.

06
Study Vendors & Engineering Teams

Senior QA/QC for SKM/ETAP models, arc flash scenario matrices, TCC reviews, and protection settings — calibrated to hyperscale architecture. We provide overflow review and independent validation for study vendors working on high-stakes data center programs.

How We Enter a Project

A Clear Starting Point for Every Engagement

Most advisory relationships start with a well-scoped, fixed-fee review. You get a clear deliverable. We get a clear line of sight into your risk. Below are the most common ways teams bring us in.

Flagship Offer · Fixed Fee
Independent Power Systems Review

A 2–3 week fixed-fee review of your power system — designed to surface protection, arc flash, and commissioning risks before they become schedule delays, failed energizations, or safety exposures. Deliverable is an executive risk memo, findings matrix, and prioritized corrective actions.

  • SKM / ETAP model assumptions and scenario coverage
  • Utility fault data validation
  • Arc flash scenario matrix and label philosophy review
  • Relay coordination and TCC gaps
  • MV/LV protection settings and miscoordination risk
  • Generator, UPS, and switchgear operating mode review
  • Commissioning and energization risk assessment

Deliverable: Executive risk memo + technical findings matrix + top corrective actions. PE-stamped where required.

Commissioning Readiness Review

Pre-energization review of test scripts, relay settings, switching sequences, and outage risk. Designed to catch field exposure before energization day — for EPCs and Cx firms.

SKM / ETAP Model & Arc Flash QA

Independent QA of existing coordination studies, arc flash models, and scenario matrices. We review what was done, what was assumed, and what was missed — then deliver a gap findings report.

Utility Interconnection / Large-Load Review

Customer-side review of utility interconnection packages, protection interface assumptions, large-load study inputs, and ERCOT-facing interconnection documentation for Texas data center projects.

Reliability & Incident Response

Rapid RCA support for electrical events — SER and relay data analysis, failure mode assessment, corrective action development, and fleet-level lessons-learned documentation.

Owner's Engineer / Technical Due Diligence

Independent electrical review for investors and owner's reps — one-line review, equipment basis-of-design, power capacity and expansion readiness, and electrical risk summary for decision-making.
