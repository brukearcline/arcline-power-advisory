Core Services

Four Practice Areas

Power System Studies & Protection Review

Independent QA and execution of arc flash, short-circuit, and coordination studies. Validation of relay settings, arc flash assumptions, and protection configurations against IEEE 1584, NFPA 70E, utility requirements, and owner-specific operating modes.

Arc Flash IEEE 1584 SKM / ETAP Relay Coordination

Commissioning & Operational Readiness Advisory

Pre-energization review, commissioning sequencing, and utility coordination for new data center builds and retrofits. Translating design packages into field-executable energization plans — with owner-side judgment on what can go wrong.

Utility Interconnection Energization Planning HV/MV Systems Field QA
