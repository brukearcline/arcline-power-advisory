Arcline is a new firm. The experience is not. The projects below represent work our founders led in prior roles at Meta, AWS, and Microsoft — the basis for the judgment we bring to every engagement.

Protection Coordination · Pacific Northwest
345kV Substation Protection Study for 120MW Hyperscale Campus

Led end-to-end protection coordination study for a new utility-owned 345kV substation serving a major hyperscale operator's flagship campus. Developed relay settings from transmission level through 34.5kV feeders to MV switchgear and generator protection. Coordinated with the serving utility's protection engineers to validate settings against NERC compliance requirements.
